# Section I — Economic Factors and Business Information

## Format: Conceptual multiple choice

### Q001

Which statement best describes the usual effect of an expansionary monetary policy?

A. It tends to reduce liquidity and increase short-term interest rates.  
B. It tends to increase liquidity and put downward pressure on short-term interest rates.  
C. It permanently eliminates inflation.  
D. It directly increases corporate tax rates.

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Expansionary monetary policy generally increases money or credit availability, which tends to lower short-term rates and stimulate economic activity. It does not guarantee the elimination of inflation.

### Q002

Which action is most directly associated with expansionary fiscal policy?

A. Increasing government spending or reducing taxes  
B. Selling securities to drain reserves  
C. Increasing reserve requirements  
D. Raising the discount rate

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Fiscal policy concerns government spending and taxation. Increasing spending or reducing taxes is generally expansionary.

### Q003

An inverted yield curve is generally described as a curve in which:

A. Long-term yields are higher than short-term yields.  
B. Short-term yields are higher than long-term yields.  
C. All maturities have exactly the same yield.  
D. Bond prices are higher than par value.

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** An inverted curve has higher short-term yields than long-term yields. It may signal expectations of slower growth or future rate declines, but it is not a certain forecast.

### Q004

If domestic inflation rises while a foreign currency's value remains unchanged, the domestic currency's purchasing power generally:

A. increases  
B. decreases  
C. becomes unrelated to prices  
D. is guaranteed to appreciate

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Inflation reduces the purchasing power of a currency when prices rise.

### Q005

Which statistic is designed to measure the change in prices paid by consumers for a basket of goods and services?

A. Gross domestic product  
B. Consumer Price Index  
C. Balance of payments  
D. Prime rate

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** The Consumer Price Index, or CPI, measures changes in consumer prices.

### Q006

Gross domestic product is best described as the:

A. total market value of final goods and services produced within a country during a period  
B. total value of all securities traded on an exchange  
C. government's annual tax revenue  
D. value of imports less exports only

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** GDP measures domestic production of final goods and services over a specified period.

### Q007

Which event is most likely to cause the market value of an existing fixed-rate bond to decline?

A. A decline in market interest rates  
B. An increase in market interest rates  
C. A decline in the bond's remaining maturity with no other change  
D. A reduction in expected inflation only if rates also decline

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Existing fixed-rate bonds become less attractive when newly issued bonds offer higher rates, so their market prices generally fall.

### Q008

A widening credit spread most directly indicates that investors are demanding:

A. less compensation for credit risk  
B. more compensation for credit risk relative to a benchmark  
C. a lower coupon on Treasury securities  
D. a guaranteed return

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A credit spread is the yield difference between a credit-risky instrument and a comparable lower-risk benchmark. A wider spread generally reflects greater perceived credit risk or risk aversion.

### Q009

Which item is normally reported on an income statement?

A. Cash balance at year-end  
B. Revenue and expenses for a period  
C. Shareholders' ownership percentage only  
D. The market value of all outstanding debt

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** An income statement reports revenues, expenses, and resulting profit or loss over a period.

### Q010

Which item is normally reported on a balance sheet?

A. Revenues earned during the quarter  
B. Operating expenses incurred during the year  
C. Assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time  
D. Cash flows from operating activities only

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** A balance sheet presents financial position at a specific date.

### Q011

Under accrual accounting, revenue is generally recognized when it is:

A. earned, even if cash has not yet been collected  
B. collected, regardless of when it was earned  
C. deposited into a bank account only  
D. approved by a securities administrator

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Accrual accounting records economic activity when earned or incurred rather than only when cash changes hands.

### Q012

Which financial statement most directly explains why a profitable company may still have limited cash?

A. Statement of cash flows  
B. Income statement only  
C. Statement of shareholders' voting rights  
D. Prospectus cover page

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** The statement of cash flows reconciles cash from operating, investing, and financing activities. Profit and cash generation can differ because of noncash items and working-capital changes.

### Q013

What is the current ratio?

A. Current assets divided by current liabilities  
B. Current liabilities divided by total equity  
C. Total debt divided by total assets  
D. Net income divided by revenue

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** The current ratio is a short-term liquidity measure: current assets ÷ current liabilities.

### Q014

If a company has current assets of $600,000 and current liabilities of $300,000, its current ratio is:

A. 0.5  
B. 1.0  
C. 2.0  
D. 3.0

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** $600,000 ÷ $300,000 = 2.0.

### Q015

A stock has a market price of $48 and earnings per share of $4. What is its price-to-earnings ratio?

A. 8  
B. 12  
C. 16  
D. 192

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** P/E = market price ÷ EPS = $48 ÷ $4 = 12.

### Q016

Which risk is most appropriately classified as systematic risk?

A. A company's factory fire  
B. A product recall affecting one issuer  
C. A broad increase in market interest rates  
D. A lawsuit against one company

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** Systematic risk affects broad markets and cannot be eliminated through diversification. The other examples are issuer-specific risks.

### Q017

The opportunity cost of choosing Investment A over Investment B is best described as:

A. the accounting fee charged for Investment A  
B. the benefit foregone by not choosing the next-best alternative  
C. the tax basis of Investment A  
D. the market value of Investment B after purchase

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Opportunity cost is the value of the next-best alternative that was not selected.

### Q018

In a corporation's liquidation, which claim generally has the highest priority among the choices below?

A. Common stock  
B. Preferred stock  
C. Secured debt  
D. Unsecured common dividends

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** Secured creditors generally have priority over unsecured creditors and equity holders, subject to the specific security and applicable law.

## Format: Quantitative multiple choice

### Q019

An investment grows from $10,000 to $10,800 in one year and pays no distributions. What is the holding-period return?

A. 0.8%  
B. 8%  
C. 10.8%  
D. 80%

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Return = ($10,800 − $10,000) ÷ $10,000 = 8%.

### Q020

An account earns 6% before inflation. If inflation is 2%, the approximate real return is:

A. 2%  
B. 4%  
C. 6%  
D. 8%

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** The common approximation is nominal return minus inflation: 6% − 2% = approximately 4%. The exact real return is slightly below 4%.

# Section II — Investment Vehicle Characteristics

## Format: Product-identification multiple choice

### Q021

Which instrument is a short-term obligation of the U.S. Treasury that is normally issued at a discount and matures at par?

A. Treasury bill  
B. Treasury bond with a semiannual coupon  
C. Corporate preferred stock  
D. Municipal revenue bond

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Treasury bills are short-term Treasury obligations generally issued at a discount and redeemed at face value.

### Q022

Commercial paper is best described as:

A. long-term secured government debt  
B. short-term unsecured corporate debt  
C. an equity interest in a money market fund  
D. a municipal bond backed by tax receipts

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Commercial paper is short-term, unsecured debt issued by corporations and other eligible entities.

### Q023

Which statement about a bank certificate of deposit is generally correct?

A. It represents common-stock ownership.  
B. It is a deposit instrument with a stated maturity and may carry an early-withdrawal penalty.  
C. It is always free of issuer credit risk.  
D. It trades at a discount like a Treasury bill in every case.

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A CD is a time deposit. Its terms include maturity and interest, and early withdrawal may incur a penalty. It still carries bank credit risk, subject to applicable deposit insurance limits.

### Q024

Which security is designed to adjust its principal value for changes in an inflation index?

A. Treasury Inflation-Protected Security  
B. Traditional fixed-rate corporate bond  
C. Perpetual preferred stock  
D. Commercial paper

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** TIPS principal is adjusted based on changes in the applicable inflation index; interest is calculated on the adjusted principal.

### Q025

The primary risk to an investor in a corporate bond is the issuer's:

A. voting dilution  
B. ability to meet interest and principal obligations  
C. obligation to pay common-stock dividends  
D. need to file a proxy statement

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Corporate bondholders face credit or default risk, in addition to interest-rate, liquidity, and other risks.

### Q026

Which municipal bond is generally supported by the issuer's taxing power?

A. General obligation bond  
B. Revenue bond  
C. Equipment trust certificate  
D. Mortgage-backed security

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** General obligation bonds are generally backed by the issuer's taxing power. Revenue bonds are generally supported by specified project or enterprise revenues.

### Q027

When market interest rates rise, the price of a long-duration bond generally:

A. falls more than the price of a short-duration bond, all else equal  
B. rises more than the price of a short-duration bond  
C. remains fixed at par  
D. becomes a stock

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Duration measures sensitivity to interest-rate changes. Greater duration generally means greater price sensitivity.

### Q028

Which bond feature gives the issuer the right to repay the bond before its stated maturity?

A. Conversion feature  
B. Call feature  
C. Put feature held by the investor  
D. Sinking-fund restriction against repayment

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A callable bond may be redeemed early by the issuer under stated terms.

### Q029

For a premium bond, which yield is most likely to be lower than its coupon rate, assuming no unusual features?

A. Current yield  
B. Yield to maturity  
C. Nominal coupon rate  
D. Stated interest rate

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A premium bond is purchased above par. The investor's return includes the gradual loss of the premium by maturity, so yield to maturity is generally below the coupon rate.

### Q030

Which statement best describes common stock?

A. It normally has a fixed maturity and mandatory interest payment.  
B. It represents an ownership interest and may include voting rights.  
C. It always has priority over secured debt in liquidation.  
D. It is never subject to market risk.

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Common stock is an equity ownership interest. Voting rights and dividend policies depend on the issuer and class of shares.

### Q031

Preferred stock generally has:

A. priority over common stock for dividends and liquidation, but usually less voting power  
B. a guaranteed maturity value from the federal government  
C. priority over all secured creditors  
D. no claim to dividends under any circumstances

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Preferred stock typically has a preference over common stock but ranks below debt and usually has limited voting rights.

### Q032

The primary purpose of fundamental equity analysis is to evaluate:

A. price and volume patterns only  
B. an issuer's financial condition, earnings, and business prospects  
C. the order book of a futures exchange only  
D. the tax status of a bondholder

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Fundamental analysis considers economic, industry, and issuer-specific information to estimate value.

### Q033

Technical analysis primarily focuses on:

A. historical price and trading-volume patterns  
B. a company's tax return only  
C. the issuer's liquidation preference only  
D. the investor's marginal tax bracket

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Technical analysis studies market data such as prices, volume, trends, and patterns.

### Q034

Under a dividend-discount model, an equity's value is most directly related to:

A. the present value of expected future dividends  
B. the face value of a Treasury bill  
C. the investor's account number  
D. the bond's call price only

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** A dividend-discount model estimates the present value of expected future dividends, using an appropriate required return.

### Q035

An initial public offering is an offering in which:

A. a public company sells newly issued shares to the public for the first time  
B. existing shareholders sell only privately to one another  
C. a closed-end fund repurchases shares at NAV  
D. a bond issuer calls outstanding debt

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** An IPO is the first public offering of an issuer's equity securities. The issuer may receive proceeds from newly issued shares.

### Q036

In a secondary offering by an existing public company, the shares sold may be:

A. only newly created shares issued by the Treasury  
B. newly issued shares, existing-holder shares, or both depending on the offering  
C. only Treasury bills  
D. only shares of a mutual fund

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A secondary offering may involve new shares, selling shareholders' shares, or both. The effect on the issuer's capital depends on the structure.

### Q037

An open-end mutual fund generally issues and redeems shares:

A. continuously at a price based on the next calculated NAV  
B. only once at the IPO price  
C. only in the secondary market at a negotiated premium  
D. only at a fixed price unrelated to portfolio value

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Open-end funds transact with the fund at forward pricing based on the next computed NAV, plus or minus applicable charges.

### Q038

A closed-end fund generally:

A. issues shares continuously and must redeem them at NAV  
B. has a fixed number of shares that trade in the secondary market  
C. cannot invest in securities  
D. is always priced exactly at NAV

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Closed-end fund shares generally trade on an exchange or other secondary market and can trade at a premium or discount to NAV.

### Q039

An exchange-traded fund is most commonly characterized by:

A. intraday trading on an exchange  
B. a prohibition on diversification  
C. a guaranteed principal value  
D. an inability to hold a portfolio of securities

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** ETFs generally trade intraday like stocks. Their market prices can differ modestly from NAV.

### Q040

Which pooled investment is generally created with a fixed portfolio and a stated termination date rather than being actively managed continuously?

A. Unit investment trust  
B. Open-end mutual fund  
C. Bank checking account  
D. Variable annuity contract only

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** A UIT generally holds a specified portfolio and terminates or liquidates according to its governing documents.

### Q041

Which statement is most accurate about a nontraded REIT?

A. It necessarily provides daily liquidity at NAV.  
B. It may have limited liquidity and substantial offering or transaction costs.  
C. It is a U.S. Treasury obligation.  
D. It cannot own real estate.

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Nontraded REITs may offer limited redemption opportunities and can have significant fees and valuation uncertainty.

### Q042

Class A mutual fund shares commonly have:

A. a front-end sales charge, sometimes reduced by breakpoints  
B. no operating expenses  
C. a guaranteed return  
D. a mandatory performance fee only

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Class A shares commonly impose a front-end load and may offer breakpoint discounts for larger purchases.

### Q043

A contingent deferred sales charge is generally paid:

A. when shares are sold during a specified period after purchase  
B. only when dividends are reinvested  
C. when a fund's NAV increases  
D. by the fund's custodian to the investor

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** A CDSC is a back-end sales charge that may apply upon redemption, often declining over time.

### Q044

A mutual fund's NAV per share is generally calculated as:

A. total assets minus liabilities, divided by shares outstanding  
B. liabilities divided by total assets  
C. market price plus the sales load  
D. dividends divided by the number of shareholders

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** NAV = (fund assets − fund liabilities) ÷ shares outstanding.

### Q045

Which statement about a Rule 12b-1 fee is most accurate?

A. It is a fee that may be used for certain distribution and shareholder-servicing expenses.  
B. It is a federal guarantee of a fund's return.  
C. It is charged only by Treasury funds.  
D. It eliminates the fund's management fee.

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** A 12b-1 fee is an asset-based fee permitted under the Investment Company Act framework for specified distribution and servicing purposes.

### Q046

Which derivative gives its holder the right, but not the obligation, to buy an underlying asset at a specified price by or on a specified date?

A. Call option  
B. Put option  
C. Futures contract  
D. Forward contract obligation only

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** A call gives the holder the right to buy; a put gives the holder the right to sell.

### Q047

The buyer of a put option generally benefits when the underlying asset's price:

A. rises sharply  
B. falls, subject to the option's terms and premium paid  
C. remains above the strike price forever  
D. becomes unrelated to the strike price

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A put increases in value as the underlying price declines, all else equal.

### Q048

Compared with an option buyer, an uncovered option writer generally faces:

A. no market risk  
B. potentially substantial or unlimited risk depending on the position  
C. a guaranteed profit equal to the premium  
D. only custodial risk

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Option writing can create significant losses; an uncovered call can have theoretically unlimited loss.

### Q049

A futures contract differs from an option because a futures contract generally:

A. gives only one party a right and no obligation  
B. creates an obligation for both parties to perform under the contract  
C. cannot be standardized  
D. has no margin requirement in any market

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A futures contract is a standardized obligation for both sides, while an option gives the holder a right and the writer an obligation.

### Q050

An exchange-traded note is generally:

A. an unsecured debt obligation of the issuing institution linked to a reference index or strategy  
B. an insured bank deposit  
C. direct ownership of every security in an index  
D. a common-stock voting certificate

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** ETNs are debt securities. Investors face issuer credit risk in addition to market risk.

### Q051

An inverse fund is designed to seek returns that generally move:

A. in the same direction as its reference index, usually with leverage  
B. opposite to its reference index for a stated period or objective  
C. exactly equal to a bank CD  
D. independently of all market movements

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Inverse funds seek opposite-direction exposure. Daily compounding can cause longer-term results to differ materially from the simple inverse of an index's return.

### Q052

Which insurance product typically has a death benefit and an investment component whose value may vary with separate-account investments?

A. Variable life insurance  
B. Term life insurance only  
C. Fixed immediate annuity only  
D. Certificate of deposit

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Variable life insurance combines insurance with an investment component whose value can fluctuate.

### Q053

A variable annuity's separate-account investments are most directly exposed to:

A. market risk  
B. a guaranteed fixed return in every contract  
C. FDIC deposit risk only  
D. no investment risk because it is an insurance product

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Variable annuity separate accounts are securities-related investments and their values can fluctuate.

### Q054

Which is a principal risk of investing in digital assets?

A. Price volatility, custody risk, operational risk, and uncertain regulatory treatment  
B. Guaranteed liquidity at par value  
C. Elimination of all fraud risk by blockchain technology  
D. Guaranteed FDIC insurance

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Digital assets can involve substantial market, custody, cybersecurity, operational, and regulatory risks.

### Q055

Compared with a diversified limited partnership, a direct investment in one commodity generally has greater:

A. issuer diversification  
B. concentration and price risk  
C. deposit insurance  
D. voting rights in a public corporation

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A concentrated commodity position is exposed to the specific commodity's price drivers and may be highly volatile.

# Section III — Client Investment Recommendations and Strategies

## Format: Client-scenario multiple choice

### Q056

Before recommending an investment strategy, the most important initial step is generally to:

A. select the highest-returning security from last year  
B. gather sufficient information about the client's objectives, financial situation, and risk tolerance  
C. recommend the adviser's preferred fund  
D. ignore the client's existing assets

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A sound recommendation requires a reasonable understanding of the client and the scope of the advisory relationship.

### Q057

A client needs a down payment in six months and cannot tolerate loss of principal. Which recommendation is most consistent with that profile?

A. A concentrated small-cap equity portfolio  
B. A highly leveraged commodity fund  
C. A liquid, low-volatility cash-equivalent strategy consistent with the client's needs  
D. A long-term private equity fund

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** A short time horizon and low risk tolerance generally favor liquidity and capital preservation, subject to the client's full circumstances.

### Q058

A client's risk tolerance is high, but the client's time horizon is only three months. What is the best conclusion?

A. High risk tolerance automatically makes any aggressive strategy suitable.  
B. Time horizon is irrelevant if the client is wealthy.  
C. Short time horizon may still limit the client's capacity to bear loss.  
D. The adviser should use margin to increase the expected return.

**Answer:** C  
**Explanation:** Risk tolerance is only one part of risk capacity. A short horizon can make recovery from a loss impossible before the funds are needed.

### Q059

Which item is a nonfinancial consideration that may appropriately affect a recommendation?

A. The adviser's office rent  
B. The client's values and preference to avoid certain industries  
C. The broker's quarterly sales contest  
D. The fund sponsor's advertising budget

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Values, attitudes, experience, and other nonfinancial factors can be relevant to a client-specific recommendation.

### Q060

A client has substantial employer stock and asks for a diversified portfolio. The most important concern is:

A. the concentration risk created by the existing position  
B. whether every new security has the same ticker symbol  
C. whether diversification is prohibited  
D. whether the employer stock is automatically risk-free

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Existing concentrated holdings must be considered when evaluating overall portfolio risk and diversification.

### Q061

Modern portfolio theory generally teaches that diversification can:

A. eliminate all market risk  
B. reduce unsystematic risk when assets are not perfectly correlated  
C. guarantee positive returns  
D. eliminate interest-rate risk from every bond

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Diversification can reduce portfolio-specific risk, but it cannot eliminate broad systematic risk.

### Q062

The capital asset pricing model generally relates an asset's expected return to:

A. its systematic risk, commonly measured by beta, plus a risk-free rate  
B. its accounting book value only  
C. its dividend payout only  
D. its custodial arrangement only

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** CAPM uses the risk-free rate, beta, and the market risk premium to estimate expected return.

### Q063

An investment with a beta of 1.4 would generally be expected to:

A. move 40% less than the market in every period  
B. have greater sensitivity to market movements than an investment with beta 1.0, all else equal  
C. have no systematic risk  
D. have a guaranteed 1.4% return

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Beta measures sensitivity to market movements. A beta of 1.4 indicates greater systematic sensitivity than beta 1.0, though actual returns vary.

### Q064

The efficient market hypothesis in its semi-strong form generally states that prices reflect:

A. only historical prices  
B. all publicly available information  
C. only information known by corporate insiders  
D. no information at all

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** Semi-strong EMH concerns the incorporation of publicly available information into market prices.

### Q065

Strategic asset allocation is best described as:

A. establishing long-term target allocations consistent with objectives and risk  
B. changing positions every minute based on market rumors  
C. investing solely in last year's best-performing asset  
D. using leverage in every account

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Strategic allocation establishes a long-term policy mix. Rebalancing may be used to restore target weights.

### Q066

Tactical asset allocation generally involves:

A. temporary deviations from long-term target allocations based on an investment view  
B. refusing to rebalance under any circumstance  
C. holding only cash permanently  
D. guaranteeing the portfolio's return

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Tactical allocation changes exposures opportunistically while generally retaining a strategic framework.

### Q067

Which strategy is most consistent with a passive investment approach?

A. Attempting to track a broad market index at relatively low cost  
B. Buying and selling based on private tips  
C. Concentrating in the adviser's favorite five stocks  
D. Trading every earnings announcement

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Passive strategies typically seek to track an index or market segment rather than outperform through frequent security selection.

### Q068

Dollar-cost averaging involves:

A. investing equal dollar amounts at regular intervals regardless of price  
B. investing only after prices rise  
C. borrowing equal amounts at regular intervals  
D. guaranteeing a lower average cost

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Dollar-cost averaging may result in purchasing more shares at lower prices and fewer at higher prices, but it does not guarantee profit or a lower overall cost.

### Q069

An income-oriented portfolio is primarily designed to emphasize:

A. current cash flow from sources such as interest and dividends  
B. maximum capital appreciation regardless of volatility  
C. avoidance of all taxable income  
D. only nonmarketable assets

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Income strategies emphasize current distributions, while still requiring analysis of credit, interest-rate, market, and other risks.

### Q070

Which behavioral bias may cause an investor to hold a losing position too long because of an unwillingness to realize a loss?

A. Loss aversion  
B. Neutrality  
C. Perfect diversification  
D. Arbitrage

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** Loss aversion is the tendency to feel losses more strongly than equivalent gains, which may distort decisions.

### Q071

A client asks for a recommendation solely because a security is “cheap” relative to its prior price. The adviser should first consider:

A. whether the security's current fundamentals and risks support the recommendation  
B. buying it immediately because all declines reverse  
C. using margin to improve the discount  
D. ignoring the client's objective

**Answer:** A  
**Explanation:** A lower price alone does not establish value or suitability. Current facts, fundamentals, risk, and client circumstances matter.

### Q072

An investor in a high marginal tax bracket is comparing a taxable bond with a tax-exempt municipal bond. The adviser should compare:

A. only the stated coupon rates  
B. after-tax yields, credit risk, maturity, liquidity, and the client's circumstances  
C. only the bond ratings  
D. only the municipal bond's tax label

**Answer:** B  
**Explanation:** A fair comparison considers taxable-equivalent or after-tax yield along with risk and suitability factors.

### Q073

A. $4,000

1. **T/F:** Diversification can eliminate systematic market risk.  
   **Answer:** False. It can reduce unsystematic risk but cannot eliminate broad market risk.

7. **T/F:** A closed-end fund can trade at a premium or discount to NAV.

   **Answer:** True.
